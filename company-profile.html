How We Work

Our Engagement Process

A clear, disciplined process from discovery to launch — and beyond.

01

Discovery & Architecture

Free initial consultation to understand goals, constraints and risk profile. We produce a written architecture document, technology recommendation, timeline and fixed cost estimate.

02

Sprint-Based Development

2-week sprints with weekly demos and a working preview environment. Code in your repository from day one. Daily Slack / WhatsApp access to the engineering lead.

03

Security & QA

Internal multi-pass audit on all smart contracts, end-to-end test suite for application code, and optional independent third-party audit before mainnet launch.

04

Launch & Ongoing Support

Production deployment with monitoring, incident response playbook and a dedicated support channel. Retainer-based support available for continuous evolution.
